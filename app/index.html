    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Six phases, in order, from the first mission to Vice Got a Price! The roadmap is built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, one-off moments when they appear, strangers before the finale, then the grinds and the cleanup.

      How long?

      Launch-day guides estimate around 200 hours for the platinum. There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies — it is long, not hard. The hardest single trophy by skill is The Shootist (gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges); the longest by time is A Long Road (100% completion).

      Watch Out — the one-off moments

      No confirmed missable list exists on launch day (19 November 2026). The flags below are derived only from the official descriptions: a trophy is flagged Watch out when its description names one specific mission, place or moment rather than a repeatable activity. Do those the first time the moment appears, and keep a manual save before it — launch-day guides report there is no mission replay.

        Sources & honesty

        Every guide in this app is written in the author's own words for this entry. It is grounded in the trophy's official description, and it cites its sources beside the guide. A specific place, step or count is only stated when the official description or a named source supports it — anything else is marked unconfirmed rather than invented.

        Where the facts come from

          What this guide does not claim

          • Three trophies name a moment without naming its trigger — Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There. Their guides say so openly instead of inventing a method.
          • Counts PSN tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 Top Fun locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 9 activity types, 500 choices) come from PSNProfiles' trophy list, which agrees with Exophase on every name, description and grade.
